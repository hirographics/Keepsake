Art templates

Layered files that open in Procreate. Each has a guide layer with the safe lines and short steps. Draw on the "ART" layer, hide the guides, export a PNG.

Get a template into Procreate

  1. On the iPad, tap a download button below.
  2. Tap the download in Safari, then tap the file to open it.
  3. Tap the share button and choose Procreate. The layers come in as they are.

Export for the app

  1. Hide the GUIDES layer. For stickers, also hide BASE if it is there.
  2. Actions (wrench) > Share > PNG.
  3. Name it with a short id, like dice-red.png.

What a scrap pack is

A folder with your PNGs and one list file, pack.json. The list names each piece and its kind: sticker, stamp, tape or paper. Stamps are one solid color on see-through; the app inks them. Send me a folder of PNGs and I will write the list file.
